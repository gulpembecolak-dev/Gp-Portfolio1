01 Packaging

BOON Luxe Koffie
Packaging Design

Premium coffee packaging with copper foil stamping on matte black board — from die-cut engineering to shelf-ready mockup.

2025Packaging · Print
View details

Case Study

Brief
BOON needed packaging that communicates craft and premium positioning for their Speciale Melange line — standing out in specialty retail against established roasters.
Approach
Matte black card stock as the foundation, with copper foil stamping for the monogram and key details. The die-cut structure uses a gable-top form — distinctive on shelf and practical for resealable use. Technical drawings were developed alongside the visual design to ensure production accuracy.
Typography & Detail
A custom-spaced slab serif for "BOON" sits above condensed sans for descriptors. Copper iconography for brew method and roast level provides functional elegance. Every panel is considered — front, side, and base carry the brand language consistently.
Role
Full packaging design: concept, die-cut development, typography, icon system, and production-ready artwork.
Tools
Adobe Illustrator · InDesign · Photoshop
02 Branding

AURA° Cosmetics
Brand Identity

Complete visual identity for a botanical skincare brand — from typography system and colour palette to product label and brand book.

2025Identity · Packaging
View details

Case Study

Brief
AURA° is a botanical skincare line seeking an identity that feels refined yet grounded — natural without cliché, premium without coldness. The brand targets women who value ingredient transparency and quiet luxury.
Approach
The identity centres on Lora Serif paired with Karla Sans — balancing sophistication with readability. The colour palette draws from natural minerals: deep charcoal, oat beige, taupe grey, cream linen, and natural stone. Gold foil accents on dark stock elevate the physical presence.
Deliverables
Brand book with typography and colour system, primary product label design (Radiance Serum), and a full guidelines document covering application across print, digital, and packaging touchpoints.
Tools
Adobe Illustrator · InDesign · Photoshop

2025Social · Art Direction

Case Study

Brief
Velvet Oak is an ethical fashion brand with an existing audience but inconsistent visual language on Instagram. They needed a grid system that tells their sustainability story while feeling aspirational and shoppable.
Approach
